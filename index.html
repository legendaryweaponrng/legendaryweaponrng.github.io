Legendary Weapon RNG

A hack-and-slash RPG on Roblox where every weapon drops from an enemy you defeated.

Legendary Weapon RNG is a free-to-play Roblox RPG made by a solo developer. You fight through areas full of enemies, and the weapons those enemies drop become your arsenal. If you want a stronger weapon, you go and take it from something stronger.

The arsenal

Every weapon below is an original design from the game — and in game, none of them are handed to you. Each one drops from an enemy. Chase them all, absorb the spares into your CORE, and keep only the best.

How it plays

  1. Fight — Battle enemies and bosses across a chain of areas, including HARD difficulty tiers.
  2. Drop — Every defeated enemy can drop a weapon. Your LUCK stat decides how good the drop is, across 20 rarities.
  3. Equip and absorb — Equip the best drops. Absorb the rest into your CORE to grow permanently stronger.
  4. Push further — Stronger weapons open stronger areas, whose enemies drop even better weapons.

Your weapons float around you and fight on their own, so the hunt continues hands-free: AFK auto-battle is a core system for every player, not a paid perk. Sessions can be active boss hunts or long idle grinds — both move you forward.

Key features

  • 500+ weapons, each with its own power — no two drops are ever exactly the same
  • 20 rarities, from common drops to ERROR at 1 in 10,000,000
  • Serial Numbers — the first player to discover a weapon gets their name recorded on it forever
  • CORE system — absorb duplicate and weaker weapons to grow stronger
  • FORGE — a second path to chase the rarest weapons
  • Daily BAZAAR — a player market for trading
  • Leaderboards — Top Power and Top Asset Value
  • Weekend events — boosted LUCK and drop rates
  • Solo developed and actively updated; saves and purchases are permanent

FAQ

How do I get weapons?
Weapons drop from defeated enemies and bosses — every weapon in your arsenal was taken from something you defeated.

What is the rarest thing in the game?
The ERROR rarity, at 1 in 10,000,000. Every weapon also carries a serial number, and the first discoverer's name stays on it permanently, so a true one-of-a-kind drop is possible.

Can I play AFK?
Yes. Your weapons fight on their own around you, and auto-battle is built into the game for everyone from the start.

Is it free to play?
Yes, completely free to play on Roblox.

Are there codes?
Yes — try code LEGENDARY in game.

Who makes it?
A solo developer (keppekicamp). Bug reports and feedback are welcome in the ZENMAI group on Roblox.
